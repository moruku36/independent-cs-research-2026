# Independent CS Research 2026

Public notes for independent researchers and industry practitioners who do computer science research and want to turn it into papers and conference talks. This repository accumulates publication pathways, candidate research topics, AI paper reading logs, and practical application ideas, all based on publicly available information.

**Status (2026-10-22 JST):** Research plans are in the initial phase. Each paper note states what was actually checked (primary text, partial read, or abstract only). Replication experiments and original-research validation have not been done. Eligibility for presentation, acceptance precedents, biographical details, and affiliation wording are still undecided.

## Paper notes by theme

Start from a theme; each folder README lists its papers with their verification status. [All themes and conventions](surveys/README.md).

- [AI agent safety and out-of-scope behavior](surveys/agent-safety/README.md) (2): Whether AI agents act outside their authorization, and how they can be attacked in real workflows.
- [Decoys, deception, and LLM system defense](surveys/deception-defense/README.md) (2): Using fake evidence and decoy models to steer or isolate malicious agents and requests.
- [Coding agents, code review, and software engineering](surveys/coding-agents/README.md) (6): Benchmarks and industrial studies of AI coding assistants, automated repair, and automated review.
- [Human-AI collaboration at work](surveys/human-ai-collaboration/README.md) (2): What people expect from AI co-workers, and how agent tools should treat the user's waiting time and attention.
- [Security governance: privacy, policy, and crypto-agility](surveys/security-governance/README.md) (2): Organizational and policy-level security questions around GenAI use and cryptographic migration.
- [LLM serving infrastructure and cost-efficiency](surveys/llm-infrastructure/README.md) (1): How to choose and schedule hardware for serving LLMs.
- [Conference and technical articles (not counted as academic papers)](surveys/articles/README.md) (4): Vendor reports, conference recaps, and company articles. Kept apart from peer-reviewed or preprint papers.

## About this repository

Published content is limited to publicly verifiable CS background, OSS contributions, research plans, and public paper information. Internal company information, private correspondence, confidential data, and non-public personal details are excluded. No biographical or degree claims are made until a public source is checked, and neither an employer nor a past university is treated as a current research affiliation.

English is the primary language. Every page has a Japanese counterpart (`*.ja.md`), linked from the switcher at the top of each page. Where the two differ, the Japanese originals are authoritative for Japan-specific details (venues, deadlines, fees).
