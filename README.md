## Reading the notes

Papers, company articles, practitioner accounts, and corporate disclosures are identified separately. Each note records what was checked, limitations, and whether reproduction was performed. Practical applications are proposals unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Research plans remain at an early stage; independent validation and replication have not been performed. Presentation eligibility, acceptance precedents, and biographical and affiliation wording remain undecided.

The repository uses public information and excludes internal company information, private correspondence, secrets, and non-public personal details. To prevent misrepresentation before publication, please refrain from reproducing, reusing, quoting, or summarizing this project's structure, claims, figures, and research findings in other media.

Use the language switch at the top of each page. English is the primary language; the Japanese version is authoritative for Japan-specific details.
