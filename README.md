## English

Public notes for independent researchers and industry practitioners working on computer science research toward paper submissions and conference presentations. This repository accumulates publication pathways, candidate research topics, AI paper reading logs, and practical application ideas based on publicly available information.

**2026-10-15 UTC Update:** Research plans are in the initial phase. Seven papers are recorded in the survey collection; primary texts have been verified for Astra, SWE-Journey, FlowAgent, GitInject, and the CAMM evaluation, and SWE-Journey remains partially read. Replication experiments and original research validations have not yet been conducted. Eligibility for presentation, acceptance precedents, biographical details, and affiliation formatting remain undecided.
