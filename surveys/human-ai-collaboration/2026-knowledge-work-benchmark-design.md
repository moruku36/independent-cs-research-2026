## Practical reading (seven lines)

1. The paper questions broad work-capability claims from benchmark scores.
2. It proposes specifying work activity, tested setting and scored work product.
3. It derives 18 activities and analyzes cases from GDPval, OfficeQA Pro and APEX-SWE.
4. Correct final answers differ from usable downstream artifacts and handoffs.
5. Claims should match measured scope; deployment still needs local evidence.
6. Proposed use: acceptance criteria covering evidence, authorized scope and handoff usability.
7. The methodology is not a trial showing improved organizational productivity.
