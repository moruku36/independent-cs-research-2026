# Survey Record Template

## Overview

Place this section first, right after the title, and write exactly three lines:

- **What it is**: the kind of source (paper, preprint, research article) and its topic, in one line.
- **What it says**: the main question or claim and its headline result, in one line.
- **Caveat**: the most important limit on reading the result (evidence type, scope, what was not checked), in one line.

Write it in your own words after reading the source, not as a copy or translation of the abstract.

## Record fields

- Status: unidentified / primary source checked / partially read / fully read / reproduced
- Title, authors, year, venue, DOI / arXiv ID, primary URL:
- Date checked and version examined:
- Research question:
- Method, data, and comparison conditions:
- Claims and evidence:
- Limitations and threats to validity:
- Comparison with related work:
- Free-to-run reproduction plan:
- Usage status: proposed / trialed / adopted (evidence URL)
- Unconfirmed points and next checks:

Do not treat a translation or copy of an abstract as a finished survey.
