- It surveys Kubernetes, CI/CD, platform engineering, FinOps, multi-cloud, and zero trust.
- The report highlights fragmented tools, configuration drift, and governance gaps.
- For practice, map platform ownership, SLOs, and security controls before adding automation.
- Test IaC and recovery workflows against the actual environment.
- Compare costs including operations, connectivity, and failure recovery.
- Its quantitative claims and source selection have not been independently audited.

## Method, evidence, and limitations

The report claims coverage of over 60 papers. Its literature selection and numerical performance claims were not independently checked; the report is not evidence that its cited sources are all peer reviewed. No replication.

Practical suggestions here are hypotheses, not demonstrated effects in another environment. Separate causes, outcomes and unknowns, and retain before/after conditions and failures.
