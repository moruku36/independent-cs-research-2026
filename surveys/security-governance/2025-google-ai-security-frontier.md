# How we’re securing the AI frontier

- Source: [original article](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/safety-security/ai-security-frontier-strategy-tools/)
- Published: 2025-10-06
- Type: Google security strategy announcement.
- Scope: primary article checked on 2026-10-30 JST; linked studies, implementations and incident claims were not independently reproduced.

## What it says

Google announces CodeMender for AI-assisted vulnerability fixes, a dedicated AI Vulnerability Reward Program, and SAIF 2.0 with an agent risk map. CodeMender's proposed patches pass automated critique before final human sign-off. Google highlights human control, limited capabilities and observable actions.

## Practical application

For internal remediation, connect vulnerability discovery, tests and human review; do not assume an AI-generated patch is correct. Use SAIF's risk map as a design-review input, and check current program scope separately before submitting reports.

## Limits and next check

This is guidance or an announcement, not an independent evaluation. Validate controls against the actual architecture and deployment; no tests or implementation were performed for this note.
