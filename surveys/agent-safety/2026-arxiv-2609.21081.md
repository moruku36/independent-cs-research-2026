# Loopjacking: Hijacking Human-in-the-Loop Approval

- Preprint: [arXiv:2609.21081v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21081v1)
- Access and verification: Primary arXiv abstract checked; full text unavailable. [Material checked](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21081). No independent replication or code review.
- Recorded: 2026-10-30 JST.

## Question and method

A person may approve operation A while a materially different operation B is executed: the approval UI may misrepresent B, or workflow state may be substituted after approval. The authors test selected product versions and report both vulnerable and resistant configurations; they explicitly do not estimate ecosystem prevalence.

## Practical application

Display canonical action parameters, bind approval to an immutable exact action, and revalidate at execution; test state mutation between review and execution.

## Limits and follow-up

Results are author-reported and apply to the studied configurations. The experiments were not independently reproduced. Check applicability to actual tools, permissions and deployment.
