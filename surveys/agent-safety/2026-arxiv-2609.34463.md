# CoDeL: Co-Evolutionary Defense against Indirect Prompt Injection in LLM-based Agents

- Preprint: [arXiv:2609.34463v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34463v1)
- Access and verification: Primary HTML: abstract and main sections checked. [Material checked](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34463v1). No independent replication or code review.
- Recorded: 2026-10-30 JST.

## Question and method

The authors train a defender while a co-evolving prober searches for attacks it still misses, including delayed deviations. The defender optimizes safety and task progress together. The abstract reports an 88.5% reduction in attack success across its experimental setup; the result is not a guarantee against new attacks.

## Practical application

Use adversarial regression tests that evolve with discovered failures, measuring legitimate task success alongside attack resistance. Keep runtime authorization and isolation even with trained defenses.

## Limits and follow-up

Results are author-reported and apply to the studied configurations. The experiments were not independently reproduced. Check applicability to actual tools, permissions and deployment.
