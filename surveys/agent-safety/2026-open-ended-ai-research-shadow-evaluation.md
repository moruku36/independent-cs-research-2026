# Can AI agents conduct open-ended AI research?

## Overview

- Source: arXiv:2607.27191v2
- Finding: The agents completed engineering work but did not substantially answer the research questions.
- Caveat: Two cases cannot establish the limits of all agents or research fields.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27191v2) · Checked October 29, 2026; key sections read, not reproduced.

## Practical reading

1. Agents tackle the open-ended questions of unpublished papers and their authors assess the results.
2. Two case studies gave agents six days and substantial compute.
3. The agents completed engineering work but did not substantially answer the research questions.
4. Judgment, creative problem-solving, backtracking, resource awareness and instruction drift were recurring limitations.
5. In practice, delegate experiments while experts retain problem selection and evaluation.
6. Two cases cannot establish the limits of all agents or research fields.

## Proposed check

Define a small pilot, acceptance criteria, and safety constraints; record outcomes and human review effort. This is a proposal, not an implemented result.
