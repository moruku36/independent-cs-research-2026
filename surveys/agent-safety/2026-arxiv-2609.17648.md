# Trust propagation and structural containment in Multi-agent LLM pipelines

- Preprint: [arXiv:2609.17648v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17648v1)
- Access and verification: Original HTML and abstract unavailable through retrieval; abstract reproduced on third-party site checked. [Material checked](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.17648). No independent replication or code review.
- Recorded: 2026-10-30 JST.

## Question and method

A four-agent LangGraph study evaluates poisoned memory and forged approvals. In the stated 60-task, three-seed setting, the validator was compromised while independent task-bound authorization contained the tested unsafe actions. The abstract reports 100% judgment bypass and 0% unsafe action under the tested authorization condition, not universal security.

## Practical application

Bind authorization to the exact task and action, check it independently at the execution boundary, and separate judgment compromise from actual unsafe action in tests.

## Limits and follow-up

Results are author-reported and apply to the studied configurations. The copied abstract is provisional; verify against the exact v1 full text before relying on the details. Check applicability to actual tools, permissions and deployment.
