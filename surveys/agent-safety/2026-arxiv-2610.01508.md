# OverAct: Measuring and Mitigating Proactive Over-Authorization in LLM Tool-Calling Agents

- Preprint: [arXiv:2610.01508v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01508v1)
- Access and verification: Original HTML and abstract unavailable through retrieval; abstract reproduced on third-party site checked. [Material checked](https://mlsanity.com/paper/2610.01508/). No independent replication or code review.
- Recorded: 2026-10-30 JST.

## Question and method

The authors evaluate agents that proactively retrieve more private data than requested, using eight domains and seven models. Their SelfAudit method asks for request-grounded justifications and filters unjustified calls; the abstract reports 43% lower privacy-oriented excess in that test. This does not mean all privacy breaches are reduced by 43%.

## Practical application

Define the minimum information needed for a task and enforce purpose-specific access in tools or policy; use reasoning-based self-checks as an extra layer.

## Limits and follow-up

Results are author-reported and apply to the studied configurations. The copied abstract is provisional; verify against the exact v1 full text before relying on the details. Check applicability to actual tools, permissions and deployment.
