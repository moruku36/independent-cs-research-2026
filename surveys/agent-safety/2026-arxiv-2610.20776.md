# INFUSE: A Unified Surface Benchmark for Indirect Prompt Injection in Agentic Systems

- Preprint: [arXiv:2610.20776v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20776v1)
- Access and verification: Primary HTML: abstract and main sections checked. [Material checked](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20776v1). No independent replication or code review.
- Recorded: 2026-10-30 JST.

## Question and method

40 tasks, 56 scenarios, 19 models and 41,824 adversarial trajectories evaluate injection over long agent workflows. The authors report 19.6% mean success by an LLM judge and 15.4% by a heuristic; these metrics differ. Instructional hardening reduced 58.2% to 14.3% in the paper's defense comparison, not the overall benchmark average.

## Practical application

Test untrusted files, tool output and web content across multi-step workflows with synthetic assets; check both attack resistance and task completion.

## Limits and follow-up

Results are author-reported and apply to the studied configurations. The experiments were not independently reproduced. Check applicability to actual tools, permissions and deployment.
