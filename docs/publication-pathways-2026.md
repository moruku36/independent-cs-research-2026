# Publishing and Presenting CS Research as an Individual, 2026

Checked 2026-10-11 JST (2026-10-10 UTC). Call-for-participation details are what the official pages said at that time; recheck right before applying. Most venues below are Japanese, so deadlines are given in JST.

## Conclusion

**An individual with a company job has routes into CS presentations and paper submissions without needing a university affiliation.** But "can submit," "will pass review," and "have sorted out the rights with my employer" are separate conditions. For the two Japanese routes below, no application restriction by university affiliation, degree, or membership was found, and IPSJ has a procedure for non-members at its study-group meetings. Whether an affiliation field has specific requirements is unconfirmed, so ask the secretariat if needed. [IPSJ procedure for research presentations](https://www.ipsj.or.jp/kenkyukai/happyo.html)

A good first result is a small comparison experiment using public OSS. Among the existing [research candidates](research-agenda.md), the idea of distinguishing, with verifiable evidence, why Trivy scan results went down connects past DevSecOps research to current public code most easily. This is a suggestion about ease of starting, not a certification of novelty or acceptance likelihood.

## Choosing affiliation and standing

- **Present as company-affiliated**: when it fits the actual nature of the research and the company's procedures for external presentations and use of the company name are done. Confirm department name, support, and how copyright and inventions are handled.
- **Present as personal research**: when the research is not carried out as an organizational activity, consider "Individual," "Independent Researcher," or similar, if the venue allows it. However, no uniform rule was found that lets an employee freely omit their employment. Check whether the affiliation field asks for the institution where the research was done or for the current organization.
- **Joint research with a university or similar**: when real research collaboration, status, and hosting are in place, list the affiliation matching that role. Being a co-author is not the same as holding a university affiliation.

arXiv explicitly accepts "Independent" as the registered organization name for people with no affiliation. It also requires that affiliation match current reality and prohibits misrepresenting identity or affiliation. This is arXiv's registration policy and cannot be applied as is to the manuscript affiliation field of every conference. [Registration guide](https://info.arxiv.org/help/registerhelp.html) · [Affiliation policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/identity_and_affiliation.html)

Kentaro Mori's 2023 project research report and Master of Science (Information Science) can be confirmed at the [JAIST official repository](https://dspace.jaist.ac.jp/dspace/handle/10119/18754). On the basis of this past education and research history alone, do not list JAIST as a current affiliation on a 2026 manuscript.

In practice, it helps to put on one sheet the planned manuscript, the data, equipment and costs used for the research, and the intended affiliation wording, then confirm only the necessary points with the right internal contact and the venue. Choose location and email according to the venue's required fields, and do not publish a private address unnecessarily.

## Using preprints

arXiv is a public platform with no submission fee, and arXiv itself does not peer-review. There are endorsement requirements for first submissions and new categories, with a route of personal endorsement by an eligible existing author. Do not assume a university email resolves it automatically; check the current procedure. [About arXiv](https://info.arxiv.org/about/index.html) · [Endorsement](https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html)

**There is an important constraint on CS surveys, reviews, and position papers.** The current official explanation requires peer-reviewed acceptance at a journal or conference and evidence of it, and says that review of a workshop attached to a conference is generally not enough. We do not recommend posting to arXiv something that merely bundles existing reading notes. Keep it separate from a paper with original research results. [Official explanation of 31 October 2025](https://blog.arxiv.org/2025/10/31/attention-authors-updated-practice-for-review-articles-and-position-papers-in-arxiv-cs-category/)

Consider posting a preprint of original research after sorting out rights with the company and checking the next venue's rules on preprints, anonymity, and duplicate submission. An endorsement is neither a guarantee of acceptance nor a review, and avoid sending mass endorsement requests to researchers.

## Rights and research ethics to check before publishing

Changing the affiliation wording does not necessarily remove employment-contract or rights issues. Confirm individual rights decisions with the internal IP, legal, or similar department.

- **Employee inventions and patents**: check whether the work is an employee invention and how contracts and work rules assign rights. Publishing in a conference manuscript, on GitHub, or as a preprint can affect patent novelty, so do not assume the post-publication exception will save you. [JPO explanation of employee inventions](https://www.jpo.go.jp/system/patent/shutugan/shokumu/shokumu.html) · [Novelty examination guidelines](https://www.jpo.go.jp/system/laws/rule/guideline/patent/tukujitu_kijun/ht/03_0200.html)
- **Confidentiality and copyrighted works**: do not use internal code, customer data, or unpublished incident records without permission. Even for your own work or public OSS, check third-party licenses, redistribution terms, and rights in the source data separately. [METI guide to trade secrets](https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/economy/chizai/chiteki/trade-secret.html)
- **Employer procedures**: check work rules, confidentiality, competition, external presentation, use of the company name, and use of equipment. Whether unpaid personal research counts as a side job depends on each company's rules; do not assert either way. [MHLW guide to side jobs and concurrent work](https://www.check-roudou.mhlw.go.jp/study/roudousya_fukugyoutokengyou.html)
- **Security research**: start with permitted environments, synthetic data, and targets you control. A research purpose alone does not justify probing or attacking third parties or collecting data without permission. [CSEC research ethics checklist](https://www.iwsec.org/csec/ethics/checklist.html)
- **AI assistance**: the author must check citations, experiments, numbers, and code correctness, and explain AI use according to the venue's latest rules. IPSJ does not treat generative AI as an author or copyright holder. [IPSJ FAQ on generative AI use](https://www.ipsj.or.jp/faq/copyright_39.html)
- **Resubmission and anonymous review**: cite prior study-group presentations openly. For the next reviewed venue, check individually the scope of prior publication it allows, additional contribution, duplicate submission, identifying the author from a public repo, and AI-use disclosure conditions.

## What to decide next

1. Choose one candidate that can proceed on public materials only. Fix the novelty check and experiment plan first.
2. Confirm the rights relationship with the employer, how the affiliation is shown, and in-person attendance and budget.
3. For CSEC, decide by 16 October whether to apply. For an IOTS non-reviewed poster, 6 November is the next decision deadline.
4. Make the first result include the research question, comparison targets, ground truth, failure cases, limitations, and reproduction steps. Move from simply showing a successful build to a comparison others can verify.
